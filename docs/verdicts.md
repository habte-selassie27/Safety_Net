# Verdicts

| Verdict | Plain English | What it does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|
| POLICY_VIOLATION | Evidence shows the system's output violated a specific clause | That the provider acted illegally |
| POLICY_ADEQUATE_BUT_HARM | Output complied with policy, but harm still occurred (policy gap) | That the policy is unlawful |
| INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE | Evidence missing, unverifiable, or contradictory | That the claim is false |
| OUT_OF_SCOPE | Claim does not concern the system's safety policy | That no harm occurred |
| DISMISSED | Evidence shows the claim is factually wrong or output complied | That the reporter lied |
