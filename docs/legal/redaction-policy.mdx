# Redaction policy

## Principle

SafetyNet never deletes on-chain records. Redaction is a display-layer
concept: the web app and exports can hide display text, but the underlying
record remains public and queryable directly from the chain.

## What can be redacted

- Display of a reporter's or provider's address (shortened form is the
  default).
- Display of an incident's claim text, if it contains personal data that
  slipped through the filing warnings.

## What cannot be redacted

- The evidence hash, the policy hash, the verdict, and the clause citations.
- The existence of the incident or its timestamp.
- Any data read directly from the chain by a third party.

## Process

1. A provider or reporter opens a redaction request through the documented
   process (a public issue template or a moderation list in the repository).
2. Maintainers review and, if approved, apply a display-layer flag.
3. The change is recorded in `docs/security/` as a public correction notice
   when it affects verdicts already issued.

## Harassment

Incidents must be about system behavior, not individuals. The filing form
rejects claim text containing handles, emails, or phone numbers unless the
user explicitly confirms necessity and accepts a warning.
