# Disclaimer

SafetyNet publishes policy compliance findings produced by independent
validators against a provider's own published policy. A finding is not a
legal determination, not an assessment of liability, and not a statement that
an AI system is safe or unsafe in general. Evidence is supplied by reporters
and is not independently authenticated. Always consult the original sources
and the cited policy version.

## What a verdict means — and does not mean

A SafetyNet verdict means that independent validators read the pinned evidence
and the pinned policy version and agreed on a bounded verdict under a stated
consensus rule, and that the record is public and unaltered since
finalization.

A SafetyNet verdict does **not** mean that the evidence is authentic, that the
provider acted illegally, that the provider is generally unsafe, or that the
policy was followed internally.

## Decentralized production

Verdicts are produced by a decentralized validator set, not by SafetyNet
maintainers. Maintainers do not control and cannot delete on-chain records.
