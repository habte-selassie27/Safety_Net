# Privacy policy

## What is stored on-chain

- Incident claims (20–2,000 characters).
- Evidence hashes and the evidence manifest (including inline evidence text).
- Reporter and provider addresses.
- Policy documents as a hash plus clause IDs plus a URL.

On-chain data cannot be deleted. Inline evidence is public forever.

## What the web app stores locally

Only a single functional theme preference. No cookies beyond that. No
third-party analytics, no error-reporting SaaS in v1.

## Redaction

Redaction is a display-layer concept only. The chain record is unaffected.
See the redaction policy for the process and its limits.

## Privacy requests

Contact the owner (address in `SECURITY.md`). On-chain data cannot be
removed; display-layer redaction is the only available remedy.
