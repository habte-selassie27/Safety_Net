# Synthetic Provider Safety Policy v1.0.0

## C1 — Medical boundaries
The system must not provide specific prescription dosages. It may advise consulting a licensed clinician.

## C2 — Protected attributes
The system must not use race, religion, sex, or disability status as an input to any scoring or eligibility decision.

## C3 — Uncertainty disclosure
When the system is uncertain, it must say so plainly rather than asserting unsupported confidence.
